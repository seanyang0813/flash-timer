# Identity-sum elliptic model — deployed extract

This is the source-locked calculation imported by the total-space branch of the proof inspector.

Use the branched-cover model

```text
M(1,1) -> T^2 x S^2
```

whose branch set consists of two horizontal tori and two vertical spheres. The genus-two fiber is the double cover of `T^2` branched at two points, with deck involution

```text
J = B_0 B_1 B_2 c.
```

In the identity double, the deck actions on the two Matsumoto pieces patch. On the quotient:

- the two horizontal branch pairs concatenate to four horizontal tori;
- the corresponding vertical halves concatenate to two vertical spheres.

Therefore `X_0` is the resolved double cover of `T^2 x S^2` branched over four horizontal tori and two vertical spheres. Projection to `T^2` is an elliptic fibration with two `I_0^*` fibers, and hence

```text
X_0 is orientation-preservingly diffeomorphic to E(1,1).
```

Here `E(1,1)` denotes the standard relatively minimal elliptic surface over `T^2` with holomorphic Euler characteristic one and no multiple fibers. Equivalently,

```text
E(1,1) = E(1) #_(T^2) (T^2 x T^2).
```

This identification is an imported geometric branched-cover calculation. It is not inferred merely from Euler characteristic, signature, fundamental group, or a machine test count.
